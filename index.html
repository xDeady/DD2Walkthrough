Before you begin

Survival rules

Dragon's Dogma 2 does not forgive inattention: a single save file, hidden timers and quests that fail silently. Read this to the end — and the game will never catch you off guard.

A single save for everything

There are no manual slots: only autosave and inn rest saves. Any mistake — the death of a quest NPC, a failed timer, a wrong choice — is recorded forever. The golden rule: sleep at an inn before any important quest — it creates a rollback point you can return to via "Load Game → Last Inn Rest".

Hidden timers

Some quests are timed, but the game never shows the timer. Time passes when you sleep at inns and houses, rest at campsites, "doze" on benches and ride oxcarts. The rules are simple:

  • Pick up a quest — do it right away, do not put it off "for later".
  • Do not sleep or sit on benches while a timed quest is active.
  • After finishing a quest objective, always talk to the quest giver to the very end — leaving the area early can mark the quest as "interrupted".

The Arisen's insurance kit

  • Wakestone — revives a dead NPC. If a quest giver dies, do not panic: find the body (city dead end up in the Charnel House) and revive them.
  • Ibrahim's shop (Checkpoint Rest Town) — buys back sold quest items and makes forgeries, which solve half of the game's "choice dilemmas".
  • Portcrystal + Ferrystone — place crystals at key points (near the Sphinx — mandatory!), saving hours of real time.
  • Struggling with a quest? Swap your pawns for fresh ones with quest knowledge — they will hint and guide you.

Three points of no return

  • "Feast of Deception" — the coronation. Afterwards part of Vernworth's affairs "freezes" and Wilhelmina's quest gets a tight availability window.
  • The summit of Moonglint Tower (quest "Legacy") — the last chance to finish ALL side affairs of the old world. At the bottom of the tower — the final sweep.
  • The Dragon's choice — attack the Dragon (true ending) or walk away (false). Both branches are described in Act IV.

Act I

Awakening

From the penal mine to the gates of Vernworth. Everything is laid down here: the first side quests, the first NPC fate branch and the first timed quest that players fail without even noticing.

1. Chained Awakening Gaoled Awakening
Type: mainLocation: penal mine
+
  1. Create your character. Attention: body build affects running speed, grab reach and carry weight; changing race/body later is nearly impossible (until NG+).
  2. Follow Fisque and talk to Rook.
  3. Bring a stone block from the mine and place it next to Rook.
  4. Defeat the gorgon outside (learn to dodge the petrification).
  5. Run to the cliff after Rook and jump.
Do not miss: before the jump, search the camp — small supplies lie in the tents and will help at the start.
2. A Tale's Beginning Tale's Beginning
Type: mainLocation: road to Borderwatch
+
  1. Follow the soldiers to the outpost, fighting off two packs of goblins on the way.
  2. At the Riftstone summon your first pawn and create your main pawn — it stays with you for the whole game.
  3. Rest at the Borderwatch Lounge.
Tip: give your main pawn a useful specialization (e.g. Woodland Wordsmith for Elvish translation — needed in two quests).
3. In Dragon's Wake In Dragon's Wake
Type: mainLocation: Melve → Vernworth
+
  1. Head to the south-west gate of the outpost and go to the ravaged Melve.
  2. Watch the vision, talk to Ulrika, Gregor and Lennart.
  3. Escort Gregor to Vernworth: goblins and harpies on the road, a cyclops in the ravine.
  4. Activate the forgotten Riftstone along the way, then take an oxcart or walk.
Do not leave Melve without picking up the side quests below: "Medicament Predicament" and "Nesting Troubles" are taken right during this quest, and "Ordeals of a New Recruit" at the outpost has a hidden timer.

Act summary

Before entering Vernworth you should have closed: Ordeals of a New Recruit, The Provisioner's Plight, Medicament Predicament, Nesting Troubles, and ideally started Claw Them Into Shape and Spellbound. Next — the capital and the densest part of the game.

Act II

Vernworth

Vermund's capital is the heart of the game. The rules work like this: Captain Brant's tasks can be done in any order, side quests revolve around them, and the coronation ('Feast of Deception') is the first point of no return.

Safe order for the act

1) Seat of the Sovran → 2) Monster Culling (Scaly Invaders, Vocation Frustration along the way) → 3) The Heel of History → 4) The Caged Magistrate → 5) Disa's Plot → 6) The Stolen Throne → 7) An Unsettling Encounter → 8) The Nameless Village → 9) The Arisen's Shadow → 10) ALL remaining Vernworth & Melve side quests → 11) Every Rose Has Its Thorn (right after the coronation!) → 12) A Veil of Gossamer Clouds → and only then to Battahl.

The Arisen's Shadow The Arisen's Shadow
Where: Vernworth, trade quarterCondition: random encounter during Brant's quests
+
ChoiceCan be missed

A hooded man — Bermudo — has been following you. Chase him down and grab him (the grab button). Then the choice:

Option A: take the bribe

Option B: refuse (recommended)

The most capricious quest in the game. The start works only like this:

  1. Right after the coronation go to the Rose Chateau and raise Wilhelmina's affinity to maximum: give bouquets (chest in her room) one per day until gifting becomes unavailable.

Act summary

Before leaving south, check the checklist: Ulrika's route, Wilhelmina, The Arisen's Shadow, Saint of the Slums, Spellbound (grimoires), Gift of the Bow / A Trial of Archery, Till Death Do Us Part. Next — Battahl, and the way back will be long.

Act III

Battahl

Orange deserts, beastren and the most dangerous timers in the game. Order is critical here: pick up Shadowed Prayers and Prey for the Pack and close them instantly — hesitation kills the Empress and a child.

Nation of the Lambent Flame Nation of the Lambent Flame
Type: mainLocation: border → Bakbattahl
+
  1. Go to Checkpoint Rest Town. Humans need a border pass (issued by Brant) or a beastren mask from Ibrahim; beastren pass freely. Trick: you can hide in an oxcart.
  2. Cross the border and follow the road to Bakbattahl.
  3. Find Menella at the Rockmouse's Burrow.
On the way through the border, do not skip Checkpoint Rest Town: The Sorcerer's Appraisal, Hunt for the Jadeite Orb, Prey for the Pack and Mercy among Thieves are taken there.
The Sorcerer's Appraisal The Sorcerer's Appraisal
Where: Checkpoint Rest Town, Myrddin's manorCondition: formal outfit (Courtly Tunic + Breeches)
+
ChoiceSorcerer mastery

Myrddin asks for the same five grimoires as Trisha (Spellbound). The solution — forgeries from Ibrahim (6,000 g per book, one day of work):

The optimal scheme

Act V

Unmoored World

The true endgame: red skies, drained seas and a limited supply of time. Ferrystones lie everywhere here, and every night of sleep brings the settlements closer to death.

The main rule of the Unmoored World

The fog advances with every rest: after several nights, settlements start dying one by one. The plan of action: evacuate all five settlements as early as possible, starting with the farthest (Volcanic Island Camp, Excavation Site), and do not sleep unless necessary. Pawns and Purgen dragons ambush you on the roads — prepare your party.

Separate line

The Sphinx: 10 riddles

The quest "A Game of Wits" starts at the Mountain Shrine (north of Checkpoint Rest Town). Main rules: do not attack the Sphinx until all riddles are done (she will fly away forever) and place a Portcrystal nearby — you will be doing a lot of running.

A Game of Wits A Game of Wits
Where: Mountain Shrine → Frontier ShrineCondition: find the Sphinx; the quest disappears after Legacy
+

The first five riddles are at the Mountain Shrine (free order). Then the Sphinx moves to the Frontier Shrine — find her there (Reunion riddle) and get four more in random order.

IRiddle of EyesReward: Wakestone

"Pass through the door and bring back that which is most precious."

Answer: in the cave by the shrine there is a chest right above the entrance — take the Sealing Phial from it and give it to the Sphinx. Do not drink the phial and do not drag other chests!

IIRiddle of MadnessReward: Portcrystal

"Bring me the one you love most of all."

Answer: place on the pedestal the character with maximum affinity. The easiest — your main pawn: hoist them onto your shoulder, set them by the pedestal and talk to the Sphinx. A Portcrystal placed here will help with the remaining riddles.

IIIRiddle of WisdomReward: 1,200 RC

"I am a lost child. Bring me my parent."

Answer: hire a pawn in the Rift nicknamed SphinxParent / SphinxMother / SphinxFather (search at the Riftstone of Fellowship — e.g. near Harve or behind the Ancient Battleground; check the creator — Capcom). Bring them to the Sphinx.

IVRiddle of ConvictionReward: duplicate of the item

"Give me that which you value most."

Answer: any item works — the Sphinx returns its copy. Give something rare and expensive: the best choice is a Portcrystal.

VRiddle of RuminationReward: 3 Ferrystones

"Where did you find your first Seeker's Token?"

Answer: return to where you picked up your very first Seeker's Token at the start of the game — a Finder's Token appears at that spot. You have 7 in-game days. Don't remember the place? Recall: for most it is the nest on the hill near Melve or the Borderwatch surroundings.

VIRiddle of ReunionReward: 100,000 g

"Seek me in my new abode."

Answer: simply find the Sphinx at the Frontier Shrine (north-west of Checkpoint Rest Town) and talk to her.

VIIRiddle of ContestReward: Ring of Ambition

A duel, but with the Ring of Derision — your damage is nearly zero.

Answer: knock the opponent off balance, pick them up and throw them off the cliff behind the columns. Remove the ring after the fight! The Ambition ring speeds up XP gain.

VIIIRiddle of DifferentiationReward: Whimsical Daydream

"I seek this person" (a portrait).

Answer: this is Dante or Vergil — two nearly identical NPCs in Checkpoint Rest Town. Dante: straight hair and an even scar (at the entrance, Vermund side); Vergil: wavy hair and a jagged scar (behind the gates, Battahl side). Check History → NPC Logbook, hoist the right one and teleport to the Sphinx.

IXRiddle of FutilityReward: Eternal Bond

"Deliver a fragile amphora to Sir Maurits in Bakbattahl." It breaks from any hit; teleporting is forbidden.

Answer: the trick — deliver not the amphora to Maurits, but Maurits to the amphora: find him at the Mural Byway in Bakbattahl, grab him and teleport to the Portcrystal at the shrine, then place him next to the amphora (not on it!). The Eternal Bond ring sharply boosts affinity with any NPC.

XRiddle of MemoryReward: Unmaking Arrow

"How many riddles have you already solved? Show me with statues."

Answer: carry exactly as many statues to the pedestal as riddles you have solved correctly (usually 9, if this is the last one). The Reunion riddle counts too.

End of the line

After the 10th riddle, attack the Sphinx and shoot her with the Unmaking Arrow: one shot = auto-save, you cannot miss! Take the Key of Sagacity from her body and open the big chest: inside is the Eternal Wakestone — mass NPC revival, the best cure for the Dragonsplague. A normal fight is pointless: at low health she flies away forever.
